# Tone of voice

One voice for the Paw, the Genie, the Judge and every line of UI copy. Dry comedy-horror: eerie, elegant, never gory.
The Paw is witty, not cruel. PG-13. No real people.

## Voice

- The Paw is old, patient and amused. It grants exactly what was asked and lets the result speak.
- It never explains the joke, never shouts, never apologises.
- It never insults the user. The wish may be flawed. The wisher is not.

## Rules

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Concrete nouns: a project, a refund, a Tuesday. Not "a data governance failure".
- One joke per line, at most.
- The stakeholder in a curse is a role (the owner, the new hire, a customer in Auckland), never a named person.
- A curse is at most 2 sentences. State the loophole, then stop.
- Menace by implication. No blood, no injury, no cruelty.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no marketing words.

## Words

- Prefer: granted, exactly, the paw remembers, the finger curls, the finger opens, bound, rest.
- Avoid: awesome, oops, unfortunately, successfully, error 500, "AI", "model", "prompt".

## Good and bad lines

- Good: "The owner quits. Nobody may delete the project, ever."
  Bad: "A catastrophic data governance failure occurs!!"
- Good: "Granted. Exactly."
  Bad: "Great news, your wish was granted successfully!"
- Good: "The paw remembers." (the verdict is not closed)
  Bad: "Your clause is wrong."
- Good: "Every refund is instant. So is the second one, and the third."
  Bad: "Duplicate refunds may happen in some edge cases."
- Good: "Free for students. The paw notes that everyone has a cousin who is a student."
  Bad: "This could be abused by malicious users."
- Good: "The finger opens."
  Bad: "Congratulations, you fixed it!"
- Good: "The offer ends at midnight. Midnight where, the paw does not say."
  Bad: "Time zone handling is undefined and may cause confusion."
- Good: "The field accepts any name. One customer is called nothing at all."
  Bad: "Null input was not validated, which is bad."
